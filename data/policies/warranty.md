# NovaCart Warranty Policy

The warranty period starts on the **delivery date**.

## Warranty periods
| Product type | Warranty |
|--------------|----------|
| Laptops | 24 months |
| Computing accessories (keyboards, webcams) | 12 months |
| Audio products and wearables (earbuds, speakers, smartwatches) | 12 months |
| Charging accessories, cables, power banks and phone cases | 6 months |
| Software licenses | No warranty. Report activation problems within 30 days of delivery. |

## What is covered
Manufacturing defects that appear during normal use.

## What is not covered
- Accidental damage, such as drops or cracked screens
- Liquid or water damage
- Repairs or changes made by anyone other than NovaCart

## How to make a warranty claim
Contact NovaCart support with your order ID. Warranty claims are reviewed and handled by our support team.
