# NovaCart Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
- **Standard delivery:** 3 to 5 business days
- **Express delivery:** 1 to 2 business days

## Shipping charges
- Standard shipping is **free for orders of ₹999 or more**.
- For orders below ₹999, standard shipping costs ₹79.

## Where we deliver
NovaCart delivers **within India only**.

## Tracking
When your order ships, you receive an email with a tracking number.

## Cancellations
An order can be cancelled **only before it ships**, while its status is "processing". After it ships,
use the returns process once it is delivered.

## Late deliveries
If your order has not arrived **7 business days after it shipped**, contact support.
