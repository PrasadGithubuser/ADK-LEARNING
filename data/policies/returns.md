# NovaCart Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
You can return an item within **30 days of the delivery date**. The 30 days start when the order is
delivered, not when it is placed.

## Condition of returned items
- Hardware can be returned **even if the box has been opened**, as long as the item is undamaged and
  comes with all its accessories and original packaging.
- Items that are damaged by the customer, or are missing parts, may be rejected.

## Items that cannot be returned
- **Software licenses and digital downloads** cannot be returned or refunded once purchased.

## How to request a return
Contact NovaCart support with your order ID and the email address used for the order, and tell us the
reason for the return. Each order can be refunded **only once**.

## Pickup
Return pickup is free of charge.

## How refunds are paid
- Approved refunds go back to the **original payment method**.
- Refunds arrive within **5 to 7 business days** after approval.
- Some refunds, especially for high-value orders, are **reviewed by our support team** before they are approved.

## Orders that are not delivered yet
A refund can only be requested after an order has been delivered. Orders that are still processing or
in transit cannot be refunded yet.
